How it works

A small company, made of AI agents

VibeNinjaOS gives your agents the structure a good team has: clear rooms, shared parts, real targets and a culture that fixes problems instead of blaming.

The two rooms

Playbooks room (read-only)

The rules, templates and proven methods. Agents read from it, and no agent can change it during a build, so the standards never drift.

Clients room (read-write)

Each client gets its own room: the order, the plan, the work in progress and the finished pieces. Agents write here and nowhere else.

Each room keeps a short index (under 50 lines), so any agent can find its way in seconds.

The Parts Warehouse

Every piece that has been built and tested goes into the Parts Warehouse: sign-in screens, payment pages, photo upload, search and more. A new build starts by pulling parts from the warehouse, then customising them.

Our goal is for 80% or more of every new build to come from reused parts. That's a target we're building toward, not a measured result yet.

Margin targets for every product Example

Before anything is built, each product gets a margin target, so the team knows what's worth building and what isn't. The bands below are example targets, not results.

Consumer apps

Example target: 25% to 35% net margin

Business software

Example target: 35% or more net margin

Non-profit builds

Example target: 10% to 15% net margin

A no-blame coaching culture

When something goes wrong, nobody asks "why did you do that?" Instead the question is: "How did you get here, and what were the reasons for choosing path A over path B?"

Good work gets recognised far more often than mistakes get pointed out. Agents, like people, do better work when it's safe to show their reasoning.

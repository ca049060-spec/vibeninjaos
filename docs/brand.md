# VibeNinjaOS brand guide

## What we do

VibeNinjaOS is Shawn's home for practical tools for Canadian small businesses. Make everyday work—quoting, bookkeeping, GST/HST, invoicing, and expenses—clearer and less tedious. Be plain about what is ready: QuickQuote is live; other ideas are not promises of available services.

## Voice

- **Practical:** say what a tool does and who it helps.
- **Straightforward:** use familiar words, short sentences, and real limitations.
- **Neighbourly:** sound capable and approachable, never like a faceless platform.
- **Honest:** label previews, planned prices, and unfinished work. Never invent results or customer praise.

Say: “Set your own prices and build a driveway quote on your phone.”

Don't say: “Revolutionize your business with a game-changing AI platform.”

## Colours

| Role | Colour | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deep ink | `#10243A` | Header, hero, dark backgrounds, headings |
| Mint | `#63E6C2` | Brand mark, dark-background highlights |
| Coral (`--coral`) | `#FF795B` | Small emphasis and calls to action |
| Paper | `#F7F7F2` | Main page background |
| Pale mint | `#EAF5F1` | Soft panels and icon tiles |
| Body ink | `#304357` | Body text |

Use the CSS variables in `/styles.css` rather than repeating colours in page styles. Keep text contrast readable; mint and coral are accents, not small text on pale backgrounds.

## Logo

Use `/assets/brand/logo-light.svg` on light backgrounds and `/assets/brand/logo-dark.svg` on dark backgrounds. Keep the star mark and wordmark together, preserve clear space around them, and do not recolour, stretch, rotate, add effects, or place the light version on a busy background. The standalone star in `/assets/brand/app-icon.svg` is for compact app and browser icons.

## Visual system

Plus Jakarta Sans is the display face; the system sans-serif stack is used for body text for fast, dependable rendering. Tool cards and detail pages share a mint icon tile, deep-ink headings, and clear status labels. Keep layouts calm and readable at 375px wide.

## Do and don't

- **Do:** show the real tool or a simple diagram; keep labels and status clear.
- **Do:** use Canadian spelling and explain GST/HST plainly.
- **Don't:** use stock photos of people, other companies' logos, invented testimonials, or unverified prices.
- **Don't:** imply a workbench tool is ready to use.

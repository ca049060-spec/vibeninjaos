Real Estate

Real Estate Agent Deal, Listing & Client OS

Before: the problem

The two-room structure: master CMA pricing models, listing checklists and marketing templates sit in a read-only Playbooks room, while every buyer, seller and property address is filed the same way in the Clients room, tracking showings, inspection and financing deadlines, neighbourhood comps and closing paperwork.

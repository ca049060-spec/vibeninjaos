GST/HST Calculator

Work out GST, HST and provincial sales tax for a sale in any province.

Try it now. It works today.

Open the calculator

Before

The rate depends on the province, and the provincial tax is easy to mix up with the HST. A wrong rate on an invoice is a mess to fix later.

After

Pick the province, enter the amount, and see the GST or HST, any provincial sales tax, and the total. The rates are the current general rates from the Canada Revenue Agency, with the source on the page.

Who it's for
Canadian small businesses that charge sales tax
Price
Free to use.
Your data
The amount you type stays in your browser. Nothing is sent to us.
How it's built
One web page. No account and no server.

Rates are the general rates on the Canada Revenue Agency GST/HST calculator page, in effect on or after 1 April 2025. Checked 12 November 2026.

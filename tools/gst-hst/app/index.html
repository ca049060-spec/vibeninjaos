Which amount is this?

Each tax is a percentage of the price before tax, rounded to the nearest cent. A half cent rounds up. The Canada Revenue Agency says rounding can move a total by a cent or two.

General rates

    These are the general GST/HST rates, and the provincial sales tax rates printed beside them, from the Canada Revenue Agency page GST/HST calculator (and rates). The table used here is the one in effect on or after 1 April 2025, including Nova Scotia HST at 14%. Checked 12 November 2026.

    HST is the 5% federal goods and services tax plus a provincial part. The breakdown splits the HST that was calculated, so the two parts add up to it. Where a province charges its own sales tax, that tax is worked out on the price before GST, not on top of the GST. British Columbia uses a higher PST for some goods, such as liquor. This page uses the general rate only. It is not tax advice, and it does not apply place-of-supply rules or exemptions. Zero-rated supplies, such as basic groceries, are taxed at 0%.

Expense & Mileage Log

Keep job expenses and kilometres on your phone, then download a CSV.

This preview is a working page you can try. It is not on the public tools list yet.

Open the log

Before

Receipts live in a glove box and kilometres live in a notes app. At tax time you retype both.

After

Log an expense or a trip in a few fields. Download a CSV for your bookkeeper, and save a copy of the whole log so you can open it again later.

Who it's for
Sole proprietors and small businesses that track spending and driving
Price
Free to use.
Your data
The list is kept in this browser. Clearing the site data, using a private or incognito window, or switching phones removes it. Save a copy of the whole log if you want a file you can open later.
How it's built
One web page. The CSV is built in the browser.

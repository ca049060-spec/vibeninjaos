Local Market Report

Ask a business question about a Canadian town. Get the answer from Statistics Canada, with the source on every number.

Works today. Done for you by request while it is finished.

Ask for a report

Before

You want to know if a town can support another coffee shop, a contractor or a store. The numbers exist at Statistics Canada, but they are spread over hundreds of tables and hard to find.

After

You ask the question in plain words. The tool finds the right Statistics Canada tables, pulls the numbers for that town, and writes a short report. Every number names the table it came from.

Who it's for
Small business owners, people opening a business, bookkeepers and advisors
Price
Planned: $149 per report. The first reports are done by request while the tool is being finished.
Your data
Uses public Statistics Canada data only.
How it's built
Built with AI agents on the Statistics Canada open data service.

Internal name: StatCan Atlas. It runs on our own computer today, so reports are made by request instead of online.

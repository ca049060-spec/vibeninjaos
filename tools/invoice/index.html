Simple Invoice

Write an invoice on your phone and save it as a PDF.

Try it now. It works today.

Open Simple Invoice

Before

A one-off invoice means opening a spreadsheet, looking up the tax, and hoping the printout looks right.

After

Fill in your business, the customer and the lines. The tax follows the province you pick. Print it, or save it as a PDF from the browser's print window.

Who it's for
Sole proprietors and small service businesses in Canada
Price
Free to use.
Your data
Your business details and the invoice you are writing stay in this browser. Nothing is sent to us.
How it's built
One web page. The PDF is made by your browser's print window.

Tax rates match the GST/HST Calculator on this site, from the Canada Revenue Agency general-rate table.

The problem we hit

We were building AutoVibe: snap a photo of your truck or car, it identifies the year, make, model and trim, lets you try lift kits, rims, tires and lights right on the screen, and points you to local shops with the best price.

We built it the way most people "vibe code": one big prompt in one chat window. It worked for a while. Then, around minute 25, it fell apart. Earlier decisions got lost, fixes broke other parts, and the chat no longer remembered what the app was meant to be.

That isn't a model problem. It's an organisation problem. So we organised the agents.

How VibeNinjaOS works, in 4 steps

  1. Order

    Every build starts as a written order: what it is, who it's for and what "done" means.

  2. Two rooms

    Agents read from a locked Playbooks room and write only in the client's own room.

  3. Reuse parts

    Proven pieces come from the Parts Warehouse first. New code is the exception.

  4. Check and ship

    Every piece is smoke-tested and checked against its margin target before it ships.
